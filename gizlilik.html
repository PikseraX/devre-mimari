Privacy Policy — Circuit Architect (Devre Mimarı)

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Devre Mimarı is an offline-playable logic puzzle game. No account registration is required and no personal data is collected on developer servers.

1. Local Data Storage

Your level progress, stars, Watt balance, language, and audio/haptics settings are stored locally on your device only.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version may display banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded video ads via Google AdMob. Users in the EEA/UK can update their consent anytime via Ad Privacy Options inside Settings.

3. In-App Purchases

Remove Ads and Watt packs are processed securely by Google Play Billing or Apple App Store (StoreKit). We never access or store payment details.
